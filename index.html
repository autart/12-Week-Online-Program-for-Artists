Why this exists

I see dozens of portfolios every week.
Barely one stands out.
What a pity.

Before an artist reaches out to a gallery, they need to be confident they have their own artistic voice — because that is what the art market actually honours. The craft is rarely what's missing. The clarity is.

When I started giving feedback on artists' practice on my social media — from the dealer's perspective — the response was overwhelming. "Nobody tells us this. Nobody else gives us this kind of feedback."

That is why this program exists. It is the conversation I would have over coffee with each of those artists, if there were time. The knowledge I have been working with for seventeen years — structured, opened up, and made available to the artists who are serious about their next step.

Konstanze Wolter Gallerist · Founder of autart

Sanford Maria

Artist · Expert in art history

Sanford Maria Wintersberger is an artist and an expert in art history. A graduate of the École nationale supérieure des beaux-arts de Paris, his practice spans painting, performance, video and installation — rooted in deep art-historical scholarship and an unmistakable feeling for cultural identity.

He brings to this program something rarely taught alongside market knowledge: art history used as a working tool — the lens that lets any artist see exactly where their work stands, and why it matters. He collaborated with e.artis contemporary for Chemnitz 2025 — European Capital of Culture.

"Understanding the past and knowing what the market does — this does not compromise your art. It sets it free."
